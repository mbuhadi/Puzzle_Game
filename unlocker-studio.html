BODY — THE SHAPE OF THE LOCK
Upload a picture and crop it to the lock's shape: a rectangle, rounded box, oval, or any shape you tap out corner by corner. Everything outside the shape becomes see-through.
BODIES
COMBINATION SETS
A set is the pictures one dial cycles through, in order. Make a set per kind of dial (numbers, symbols, colours…).
ARROWS / SWIPE BUTTONS
The buttons that turn a dial. Draw "next" pointing up; add a separate "previous" picture or let it be the same picture upside down. Left/right dials turn them sideways automatically.
SUBMIT BUTTONS
The button that checks the combination. Optionally add a "pressed" picture shown while it's held down.
